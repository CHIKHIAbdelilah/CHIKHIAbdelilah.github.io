Centrifugal Compressor — Aerodynamic Analysis

Preliminary impeller design, including hub and shroud contours, blade geometry, backsweep, and tip clearance. Mesh generation and CFD simulation to assess pressure ratio, efficiency, and aerodynamic losses.

Ahmed Body — External Aerodynamics

Simulation of turbulent flow around the Ahmed body using the k–ω SST turbulence model. Assessment of drag coefficient, near-wall resolution (y+), convergence, and mesh sensitivity, followed by analysis of the wake and vortical structures.
