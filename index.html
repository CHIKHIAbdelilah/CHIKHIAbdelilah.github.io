Compressible flow / Turbomachinery

NASA Rotor 37

ANSYS CFX · CFD-Post · 2026

Objective: assess a transonic compressor’s performance.
My work: simulated the rotor flow and analysed Mach distributions, shock waves, blade-tip flow and aerodynamic losses.

88.58%
Total-to-total efficiency
1.889

19.64 kg/s
Mass flow rate

Against NASA reference values: −2.74% in mass flow and −10.32% in pressure ratio. These differences remain part of the analysis.

Method & technical findings
  • Compressible-flow simulation through an axial compressor rotor.
  • Post-processing of pressure, velocity and relative Mach number distributions.
  • Investigation of shock waves, tip-clearance flow and aerodynamic loss mechanisms.
  • Performance comparison with NASA reference data: the calculated pressure ratio is lower than the reference.

Incompressible flow / External aerodynamics

Ahmed Body

ANSYS Fluent · SpaceClaim · SST k–ω · 2026

Objective: investigate separation, wake development and drag.
My work: prepared the domain and locally refined mesh, ran a steady RANS calculation and analysed the flow field.

0.322
Calculated drag coefficient
0.289
Literature comparison value
+11.4%
Difference from literature

Preliminary comparison with Tian et al. (2017). Geometry, coefficient definitions and mesh sensitivity require further investigation.

Method & interpretation
  • Geometry and domain preparation with local mesh refinement.
  • Steady RANS simulation using the SST k–ω turbulence model.
  • Post-processing of velocity contours, separation and the downstream wake.
  • The drag discrepancy is reported explicitly; mesh independence has not been established.
